## Method
`variants/synth9/synth9.cpp` extends the R7 synthesis with inverse arithmetic matching: given target v and available operand a, reverse to intermediate m, then either reverse through another available b into one-op dictionary D (three-op chain), or match two D entries to construct m (up to four operations). Search uses positive bounded integers, exact division, dependency exclusions, deterministic sampled dictionary and 80000/120000 probe budgets per eligible root. Not exhaustive; no 5-op, uphill move, or register-state joint search implemented. New route is an extra candidate, not forced replacement. R8 root files and fingerprints remain unchanged.
