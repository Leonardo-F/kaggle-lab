## Algorithm
- Every number is a vertex, every exact legal binary instruction is a directed hyperedge with two operands. Combine the three fresh graphs; enumerate all valid operand pairs within their combined value set, permitting new derivations of existing vertices.
- Hub ablation: find inverse images of targets and frequently referenced nodes, intersect with one-step forward constructions, retain at most 400 new vertices ranked by number of distinct supported outputs, up to four producing recipes per hub.
- 24 large-neighborhood iterations: retain the dependency closure of a random subset of targets, erase the others, and reconstruct through a positive-cost hypergraph Dijkstra recurrence. Existing nodes cost zero; new edges cost one plus a deterministic exploration perturbation. Select graphs by distinct node count and compile using the register-aware scheduler. All routes and the final portfolio are independently replayed. This is a heuristic, not an exact globally optimal hypergraph solver: additive distances double-count common dependencies, the hub pool is capped, and graph score can differ from actual instruction count due to recomputation.
