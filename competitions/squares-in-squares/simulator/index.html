01 / GEOMETRY

Separating-axis validation

Rotated squares are checked on all four edge-normal axes, with spatial hashing to prune distant pairs. This is a local numerical check, not Kaggle’s final certified verifier.

02 / SCORING

Minimize bounding extent

The objective is the sum over puzzles of L/√n − 1. The lower bound is zero, reached by perfect-square grids. Invalid layouts receive the baseline penalty.

03 / SUBMISSIONS

Six official columns

Each row stores id, n, square_id, x, y and angle (radians). Exported values are plain decimals; independently verify the final file with Kaggle’s metric.
